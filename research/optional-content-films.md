Films are embedded from YouTube, never downloaded or rehosted. Thumbnails are
served by YouTube only after the story is opened. A deliberate play action
loads the privacy-enhanced player. Closing the story unmounts it and reopening
returns to the cover rather than resuming playback. An external viewing link
remains available if embedding is blocked. English captions are not promised;
the player exposes the caption options supplied by the publisher/platform.
Actual playback and caption availability have not been browser-tested.
